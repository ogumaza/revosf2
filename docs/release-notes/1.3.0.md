revosf2 1.3.0 converts each piece of Mega Man 9's music as one. The game plays a piece as four sequences, one for each channel of the NES's sound chip. Conversions of the other games are as with 1.2.1.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar` or `.baa` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## New in 1.3.0

- **Pieces of several sequences.** Mega Man 9 (WiiWare) plays each piece of its music as four sequences, one for each channel of the NES's sound chip. The game starts the four together. revosf2 now converts each of the game's 33 pieces to one MIDI file, one SoundFont and one set of SFZ files, named after the piece. The MIDI tracks and the SFZ files take the names of the sequences and their tracks, such as `<name>_S1 Track 0`. A piece takes the tempo of its first sequence. Its loop starts on the first pass that all four repeat. `--list` lists the pieces. `--only` picks a piece by its name or by the name of any of its sequences.
- **The README's notes on players.** They name BASSMIDI's departures from the specification as well as FluidSynth's.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 1.10.0, with seqlib 1.2.0 and xsflib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

- While a sound effect holds one of the NES's channels, Mega Man 9 silences the music's sequence for that channel. The conversion plays the music without sound effects.
- The rest are as in 1.2.1. See "JAudio games" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
