revosf2 1.5.0 converts Luigi's voice in Super Mario Galaxy and Super Mario Galaxy 2. When Luigi is the player, the games play the player's voice effects with Luigi's waves. Each voice that picks its waves by the player now converts a second time, as Luigi's voice. Every other sound converts to the same files as in 1.4.0.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.4.0

- **Luigi's voice in the Galaxy games.** When Luigi is the player, the games start each of the player's voice effects (`SE_PV_`) with a value at a port that picks Luigi's waves in place of Mario's. revosf2 now converts each voice that reads the port a second time, with `_Luigi` at the end of its name: 75 of Galaxy's 87 voices and 120 of Galaxy 2's 133. Three of Galaxy 2's voices play nothing for Luigi, and their files hold no notes. The README describes the variants under "JAudio games".
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.3.0. xsflib stays at 1.8.0 and seqlib at 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.4.0. The Galaxy games' other effects that take values from play still convert with those values at 0. See "Differences from the game" and "JAudio games" in the README for the full list.
