A release with fixes for Animal Crossing: City Folk and for the JAudio2 games. Given the game's `main.dol`, revosf2 now converts the gyroids' sounds with notes. The sound effects of Twilight Princess and Link's Crossbow Training now play their notes on the DSP's built-in oscillators. In both games, a note now ends where the game's volume falls silent. Every other sequence of a sound archive converts to the same files as in 1.3.2.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar` or `.baa` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.3.2

- **City Folk's gyroids.** The gyroids' sounds (`SEQ_HNW_*`) play in step with the music. The game counts the music's steps. A gyroid's sound plays a step each time the count changes. revosf2 1.3.2 never changed the count. Those sounds therefore converted with no notes. Given the game's `main.dol` (the USA release), revosf2 now plays one step of each sound: the count changes one frame after the sound starts. Without `main.dol`, the sounds convert as in 1.3.2.
- **The DSP's built-in oscillators.** JAudio2 plays programs 0xf0 and up on the DSP's built-in oscillators. revosf2 used to leave every note on them out. The square wave, the sawtooth, the pulse of 25% duty, the triangle and the sine now become looping samples of one cycle, one for each program. Each preset of such a program has one zone, for every key and velocity. The zone holds the oscillators' envelope: full volume until the release, then a fall to silence over about 17 ms. Sound effects of both games play such notes. Some of those effects used to convert with no notes. The DSP changes its noise and its ramp as it plays. No sample can hold them. revosf2 still leaves out the notes on them and reports them. The README describes the oscillators' samples.
- **Notes that fall silent.** A JAudio2 note's volume can fall to zero and hold there until the note's release. The game's channel is silent from there. The MIDI note used to go on to the release. The SoundFont's fitted decay could then ring on for seconds where the game was silent. The note now ends where its volume holds at zero and no release can raise it. Pieces of music and sound effects of both games change.
- **The report.** revosf2 cuts a SoundFont's level where an instrument plays above full level. The report now names the cut only when it shows as 0.1 dB or more. It used to name cuts of 0.0 dB too.
- **The README.** The SFZ section names a fourth quirk of sfizz 1.2.3. sfizz pans a mono sample by the sin/cos law and then raises both sides by 3 dB. Every sample of the SFZ files is mono. sfizz therefore plays every region 3.0 dB above the game's level, at every pan. The balance of the mix stays the same. Lowering sfizz's volume by 3 dB gives the game's level.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 2.0.0 and xsflib 1.6.0, with seqlib 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.3.2, apart from the gyroids' sounds and the DSP's oscillators. In the game, a gyroid plays a step at each step of the music. The files hold the first step only. revosf2 leaves out the notes on the DSP's noise, its ramp and the other oscillators that no sample can hold. See "JAudio games" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
