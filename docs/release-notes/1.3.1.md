A release with fixes. revosf2 now writes the SoundFonts' attenuation and pans for the way most SoundFont players read them, and the SFZ files follow the documentation of their opcodes. An archive with many sounds converts faster.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar` or `.baa` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.3.0

- **Attenuation.** FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and the E-mu chips apply 0.4 of a zone's initial attenuation. The SoundFont specification calls for all of it. revosf2 now writes 2.5 times a zone's attenuation in the generator, up to the generator's limit of 1440 cB. Those players play that limit as 57.6 dB. A modulator without a source holds any attenuation beyond 57.6 dB. FluidSynth 2.4.5 and later apply that modulator. BASSMIDI and earlier versions of FluidSynth ignore it. A player that follows the specification plays every attenuated zone too quietly.
- **Pans.** Each instrument's global zone holds a modulator from CC10 to the pan with an amount of 500. It takes the place of the default modulator. The SoundFont specification gives the default an amount of 1000. FluidSynth, BASSMIDI and most other SoundFont players use 500. A player that follows the specification now pans the notes as they do.
- **SFZ opcodes.** The SFZ files now follow the documentation of their opcodes at sfzformat.com. Bend ranges are in whole cents. A velocity range that started at 0 now starts at 1. A velocity of 0 is a note-off. The curves that move the cutoffs and resonances by controller stay within the documented ranges. They move the filters as before. The README lists the exceptions that remain.
- **SFZ decays and releases.** Their times now follow the documentation's curve. That curve falls by a factor of e^-8 (69.49 dB) over the given time. The times used to follow sfizz's curve. That curve falls by a factor of e^-9 (78.17 dB). sfizz 1.2.3 therefore ends decays and releases sooner than the game. Where the game has fallen 40 dB, sfizz has fallen 45 dB.
- **Speed.** revosf2 1.3.0 searched an archive for pieces of several sequences once for each sequence that it converted. The search now runs once for each archive. An archive with many sounds converts faster.
- **The README.** The SFZ section names sfizz 1.2 as the player tested. It lists the exceptions to the documentation of the opcodes, and sfizz's faster decays and releases. The notes on SoundFont players describe the attenuation that revosf2 writes, and how those players read the resonance of the biquad low-pass.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 1.11.0, with seqlib 1.3.0 and xsflib 1.5.0.
