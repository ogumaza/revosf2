revosf2 1.4.0 converts four more games that play their sound through JAudio: New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, Super Mario Galaxy, Super Mario Galaxy 2, and New Play Control! Pikmin and Pikmin 2. It also converts music that games play in more than one state. A piece converts once in the state that the game starts it in, and once more in each other state. Given a game's `main.dol`, revosf2 follows the code of Wii Play, Wii Music, Wii Sports, Mario Kart Wii, WarioWare: Smooth Moves, City Folk and the Mii and Wii Shop channels.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.3.3

- **Variants.** A piece that a game plays with other tracks in other states now converts once in the state that the game starts it in, and once more in each other state. A variant's files add the state's name to the piece's name. In the MIDI files, a silenced track keeps its notes at CC11 0. revosf2 notes when it plays an archive by a game's code.
- **Wii Play.** Given the game's `main.dol` (USA Rev 1 or Europe), Pose Mii's music plays only its first track. Table Tennis, Laser Hockey, Billiards, Find Mii and Tanks convert with the tracks that sound as each minigame starts its music, and once more for each later set of tracks.
- **Wii Music.** Given the game's `main.dol` (USA or Europe), the main menu's music converts in the state that the menu starts in, and once more in each of three other states. Pitch Perfect's beats convert with the tracks of the lowest levels, and once more for each other set of levels, with and without the track that joins near the end of a round. Pitch Perfect's phrases and the jingles of its pictures now send to the reverb as in the game.
- **Animal Crossing: City Folk.** Without `--weather` or `--museum`, the outdoor and town music now also converts in rain and in snow, and the museum's music in its arrangements 2 to 6. Either option converts one arrangement alone, as before.
- **Mario Kart Wii.** Given the game's `main.dol` (USA or Europe), the music of the menus and of the Wi-Fi menus converts with the tracks that the menus start it with, and once more in each other state of the menus. Six sound effects start with the value that the game gives their variable 0 at their first play.
- **Wii Sports.** Given the game's `main.dol` (USA Rev 1), golf's wind plays at a speed of 8. It converted with no notes before. `--var 0=N` and `--var 1=N` give another speed and direction.
- **WarioWare: Smooth Moves.** Given the game's `main.dol` (USA), each microgame's music plays with the reverb of the stage that plays it first. The music of the stages without a reverb gets CC91 0.
- **The Mii and Wii Shop channels.** Given the Mii Channel's title (World, version 6), its music plays the tracks that the channel starts it with. It converts once more in each of three other sets of tracks. The channel's other sounds send to its reverb. Neither channel puts an effect on the second effect bus. CC93 is therefore 0 in both.
- **Builds without a banner.** A build of the sound library without a version banner now plays as the builds of 2006, whatever AX comes with it. The Mii, Forecast and News channels link such a build. Their music now plays at the right tempo. The number of voices follows AX.
- **Super Mario Galaxy and Super Mario Galaxy 2.** revosf2 converts both games' music and sound effects. It reads their start-up archives and sequences compressed with Yaz0. Given the USA releases, it follows the rules in the code: muted tracks, the beat track, the volumes and the stand-ins of the multi BGMs. The README describes them under "JAudio games".
- **New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat.** revosf2 converts the game's music and sound effects. Given the USA release, each piece starts with the tracks that the game starts it with, without the beat track. Six effects start with the first value that the game gives them.
- **Twilight Princess and Link's Crossbow Training.** The categories of sound effects play at the volumes that each game gives them at start-up. Five pieces leave out the tracks that the game mutes as it starts them. Hyrule Field's day music converts once while Link walks and once while he rides. The states that Twilight Princess gives 17 pieces later, in its scenes, fights and field, convert as more files of each piece. Three pieces of Link's Crossbow Training get their states the same way. These rules hold for every Wii release of Twilight Princess and for the USA (revision 1), European and Japanese releases of Link's Crossbow Training. A file of a sound that sends to the games' echo now ends when the echo falls silent.
- **New Play Control! Pikmin and Pikmin 2.** revosf2 converts both games. They play their sound through the first JAudio. Give it Pikmin as the game, and Pikmin 2 as the game or its start-up archive (`.aaf`). Given the USA releases, it follows the rules in the code. Pikmin's sound effects play as the game's catalogue starts them, and the cutscenes' music converts in parts. Pikmin's tracks play at the game's volumes, and every note at the level of the game's mix. Pikmin 2's 48 pieces play as the game's sound layer plays them: with the game's answers to their calls, without the directors' tracks, with the first conductor of each cave's family and at the volumes in the game's list. Waves in 2-bit ADPCM decode.
- **Track filters.** A music track's IIR filter now becomes a pair of zones for each region: one unfiltered and one through a low-pass, at levels fitted to the filter's curve. revosf2 reports the filters that it leaves out.
- **Sequences that end.** A converted sequence that ends now ends at its last sound. It used to run on through silent waits.
- **The README.** The SFZ section names a fifth quirk of sfizz 1.2.3: it makes each decay and release last at least 6 ms.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 3.2.0 and xsflib 1.8.0. seqlib stays at 1.3.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.3.3, apart from what the changes above fix. A variant plays one state from the start. The game's fades from one state to another have no file. Some states play a track at part of its volume, such as the carousel of Wii Music's main menu. The files play it at full level. See "Differences from the game" and "JAudio games" in the README for the full list.
