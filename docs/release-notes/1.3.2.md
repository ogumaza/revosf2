A release with two new options for Animal Crossing: City Folk. `--weather` plays the game's hourly and town music in rain or in snow. `--museum` plays the museum's other arrangements. Without the options, revosf2 converts every archive as 1.3.1 does.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar` or `.baa` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.3.1

- **City Folk's weather.** City Folk plays each hourly piece and each piece of town music with one of three sets of tracks: one for fine weather, one for rain and one for snow. The game silences the piece's other tracks. revosf2 1.3.1 played the set for fine weather only. `--weather rain` and `--weather snow` now play the other two sets. `--weather fine` is the default.
- **City Folk's museum.** The museum's music has seven arrangements. The game picks one by room and starts the music in the first. revosf2 1.3.1 played the first only. `--museum 2` to `--museum 7` now play the others. Arrangements 2 and 3 play the same tracks. `--museum 1` is the default.
- **The note on City Folk.** The note that revosf2 prints for City Folk's archive now names the weather and the museum's arrangement that the conversion plays. Both options change nothing in another game's archive. revosf2 notes that when either is given.
- **Errors.** `--weather` takes only `fine`, `rain` or `snow`. `--museum` takes only 1 to 7. revosf2 stops with an error for any other value.

## Known limitations

As in 1.3.1, apart from City Folk's other arrangements. `--weather` and `--museum` convert them. See "JAudio games" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
