revosf2 1.7.0 converts more of the music that the JAudio games change as play goes on: the chord layers of New Play Control! Donkey Kong Jungle Beat, the later states of Super Mario Galaxy and Super Mario Galaxy 2, the sets of tracks of New Play Control! Pikmin, and the bosses' parts of New Play Control! Pikmin 2.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar`, `.baa` or `.aaf` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app accepts only `.brsar`, `.baa` and `.aaf` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Changes since 1.6.0

- **Jungle Beat's chord layers.** The game plays dozens of effects of its instruments in the chords of the music, and the game's code works out the keys of their notes as the music plays. Enemies and other objects of the stages loop 16 of those effects beside the stage music. revosf2 now follows the game's code for them. It converts the music of each stage once more with each such effect, and the music of two boss fights, JA_BGM_BOSS_FINAL and JA_BGM_BOSS_GOROPY_B, too: 135 files in all. A file is named after the piece and the effect, such as `JA_BGM_MAIN_01_enemy_sleeping`. The effect starts with the piece, plays its steps on the beats of the music and follows its chords for the keys. These files require the game's whole `AudioRes/Info` folder beside the folders of the start-up archive. Without it, revosf2 leaves the files out and says so.
- **Jungle Beat's effect volumes.** At start-up, the game raises the volumes of its categories of effects to between 1.1 and 1.5 times the volume in the sound table. revosf2 now does the same, for the chord effects of the layers too. The music stays as in 1.6.0.
- **The Galaxy games' later states.** As play goes on, Super Mario Galaxy and Super Mario Galaxy 2 put some pieces in other states: the Star Ball's speeds, riding Yoshi, a Dash Pepper or a Blimp Fruit, Bowser's escape, a galaxy picked on the dome's map, and others. A state turns tracks on or off. Some states also change the tempo, the pitch of the tracks or the volume of one track. revosf2 now converts each state that sounds different once more, with the name of the state at the end of the piece's name, such as `BGM_TAMAKORO_speed_very_high`: 20 states of Galaxy's pieces and 35 of Galaxy 2's. A name with `_layer_` belongs to a piece that the game plays with a stream. Each file plays the state from the start of the piece. In the game, the piece moves into the state over up to three seconds.
- **Pikmin's sets of tracks.** New Play Control! Pikmin starts five pieces with some of their tracks off. The game plays other sets of tracks while an enemy is near and in the evening. revosf2 now converts each of these pieces once more for each of the other sets, with `_enemy_near`, `_evening` or `_evening_enemy_near` at the end of its name. 1.6.0 converted each piece as it starts only.
- **Pikmin 2's boss parts.** A boss's actions make its music jump to other parts. The game's list of pieces names 13 parts of m_boss and 13 of l_boss. The files `m_boss` and `l_boss` play the parts that the bosses start at, as before. revosf2 now converts each other part as one more file, named after the piece with a hyphen and the number of the part, such as `m_boss-0`. While a captain stands near the final boss, the game turns on track 15 of l_boss. Only parts 4 and 11 have notes on that track. They convert once more with it, as `l_boss-4-near` and `l_boss-11-near`.
- **Pikmin 2's echo.** The conductor of a cave's piece sets the send of each track to the game's echo. CC91 now contains that send. 1.6.0 played the cave music dry. A file that sends to the echo now lasts until the echo falls silent.
- **Stereo waves in balance mode.** The SoundFonts used to scale the samples of a stereo wave in balance mode by 0.7071 (3 dB). The samples now keep the wave's points, and each zone sets the level of its channel as attenuation. A note on such a wave that pans toward one side used to play its near channel up to 3 dB too quietly. It now plays at the game's level. The MIDI and SFZ files stay the same.
- **Nested archives in titles.** revosf2 now finds the sound archives in U8 archives inside the archives of a WiiWare title, down to three levels. It also finds them in data compressed with the LH and LRC formats of the CX library.
- **The README.** It describes the new files and the volumes of Jungle Beat's effects. Its opening paragraph now names JAudio beside JAudio2. Much of its text is reworded for clarity.
- **WADs without their extension.** revosf2 used to read a WAD without the `.wad` extension as a sound archive, and the conversion failed. It now recognizes the WAD by its header and says what to give it instead: the title's TMD and decrypted contents.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on ntdlib 7.0.0, xsflib 1.12.0 and seqlib 1.5.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.6.0. The Galaxy games' other effects that take values from play still convert with those values at 0. Each state of a piece plays from the start of the piece, and the game's moves from one state to the next have no file. See "Differences from the game" and "JAudio games" in the README for the full list.
