A release with fixes. revosf2 plays Animal Crossing: City Folk's music as the game sets it up. It follows the rules of the sound library's builds from September 2009 and the other converters' rules for loops. Three fixes to the conversion of JAudio2 games change some of Twilight Princess's and Link's Crossbow Training's sound effects.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or later, with no additional runtime DLLs. Drop one or more `.brsar` or `.baa` files, game folders or disc images onto `revosf2.exe`, or use a terminal. `revosf2 --help` lists the options. Output goes in a folder beside each input.

For macOS on Apple Silicon, follow the build instructions in the README. macOS builds include `Revosf2.app`. Drop archives onto the app in Finder to convert them. The app takes only `.brsar` and `.baa` files. Convert games from a terminal.

## Fixes

- **Animal Crossing: City Folk's music.** The game sets up each piece as it starts. Each hourly piece and each piece of town music plays the tracks that the weather selects. The game silences the piece's other tracks. Given the game's `main.dol` (the USA release), revosf2 now plays the tracks that the game plays in fine weather. In the MIDI files, a silenced track keeps its notes at CC11 0. The museum's music plays the tracks of the room that the game starts it in. CC91 now also holds the reverb sends that the game's code gives the music. revosf2 notes when it plays an archive this way.
- **The builds from September 2009.** NW4R SND's builds from September 2009 move a time-prefixed pitch bend to its value over the prefix's ticks. revosf2 played such a bend as a jump. It now plays the move. Those builds also let a note's volume and envelope pass full level. revosf2 no longer reports a reduction for them.
- **The main loop.** revosf2 now finds loops by the other converters' rules. Inside a subroutine, a jump back isn't a loop when its pass played no note that can be heard and could leave the loop. A sequence whose tracks all reach their end has no main loop. The README describes these rules and two more.
- **A jump to the next command.** In a JAudio2 game, revosf2 counted a jump to the command right after it as a jump back. The loop markers of a sequence with such a jump could then go in the wrong place. The game's player just moves on at such a jump. revosf2 now does the same.
- **Envelope scaling.** JAudio2's command 0xe7 scales a track's envelope. revosf2 ignored it. The tracks that use it now play at the game's level.
- **The DSP's pitch limit.** The game holds a voice's pitch at the DSP's limit. In JAudio2 games, notes above the limit now play at the limit in the SoundFonts and SFZ files too.

## Other changes

- **SoundFont padding.** A sample's unrolled loop is followed by 32 samples, not 8 as the README said.
- **The libraries.** revosf2 now builds on seqlib 1.2.0, xsflib 1.3.0 and ntdlib 1.8.0.

## Known limitations

As in 1.2.0, apart from the fixes above. City Folk's music plays in fine weather. Rain and snow play other sets of tracks. See "JAudio games" and "Differences from the game" in the README for the full list.
